Privacy Policy for Trade Lock

Last updated: October 26, 2026

1. Overview

Trade Lock is a browser extension and discipline utility designed to prevent overtrading by enforcing trading rules, profit/loss targets, and lockout schedules. We prioritize user privacy and follow a strict zero-data-collection philosophy.

2. Data Collection & Storage

Trade Lock does not collect, transmit, sell, or monitor personal user data.

  • Local Storage: All user preferences, trading rules, discipline triggers, and daily trade journal logs are stored exclusively on your device using chrome.storage.local.
  • No Personal Identification: We do not collect your name, email address, IP address, or browsing history.
  • No Financial / Broker Credentials: Trade Lock does not have access to or store your broker passwords, PINs, bank accounts, or financial credentials.

3. Remote Code & Scripts

Trade Lock complies strictly with Google Chrome Web Store Manifest V3 policies. The extension does not load, execute, or evaluate external remote code, eval statements, or third-party tracking scripts.

4. Third-Party Services

The optional cloud licensing feature validates active subscription licenses via secure HTTPS requests without transmitting your trading history, browser activity, or personal documents.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact the developer via the official Chrome Web Store item listing support page.
