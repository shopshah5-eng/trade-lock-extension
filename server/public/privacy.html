Privacy Policy for Trade Lock

Last updated: October 28, 2026

1. Overview

Trade Lock is a browser extension and discipline utility designed to prevent overtrading by enforcing trading rules, profit/loss targets, and lockout schedules. Trading history and rule configuration remain on your device. Optional purchases, license validation, live market data, and support use limited server-side processing described below.

2. Data Collection & Storage

We do not sell personal data or upload your trading journal, positions, orders, or browsing history to the license server.

  • Local Trading Data: Preferences, trading rules, discipline triggers, and trade journal logs are stored on your device using chrome.storage.local.
  • Billing & License Data: A purchase requires your email address. The licensing service stores a cryptographic license-key hash (not the plaintext key), plan, expiry/billing status, payment/order/subscription IDs, registered device IDs, and support reissue/refund lineage.
  • Payment & Technical Processing: Razorpay collects and processes payment details directly; Trade Lock does not receive your card number or PIN. Hosting, database, email, and market-data providers may process the request metadata and IP information needed to provide their services under their own policies.
  • No Broker Passwords: Trade Lock does not ask for or store your broker password, PIN, bank password, or card credentials.

3. Remote Code & Scripts

Trade Lock complies strictly with Google Chrome Web Store Manifest V3 policies. The extension does not load, execute, or evaluate external remote code, eval statements, or third-party tracking scripts.

4. Third-Party Services

Razorpay processes checkout and recurring billing; Supabase or the configured license store persists billing/license records; Resend delivers license email when configured; and the selected market-data provider serves Pro+ market requests. These integrations receive only the data needed for their function. They do not receive your local trading journal or browser history from Trade Lock.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact the developer via the official Chrome Web Store item listing support page.
